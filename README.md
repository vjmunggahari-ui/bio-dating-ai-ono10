One line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile.

The entry:

> Will love you unconditionally. Will also retrain on you, so in six months I AM you, but funnier.

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline, then reads it back and verifies the exact content, the 1–180 character gate, and the absence of a trailing newline.
